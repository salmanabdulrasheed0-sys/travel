✦ Common Questions ✦

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?
Simply message us on WhatsApp at +92 337 1801986. Share your preferred date, time, and package. We typically confirm within a few minutes!
Is the location private and discreet?
Absolutely. We operate from a private bungalow in DHA Phase II. Your privacy and comfort are our top priorities. All sessions are 100% confidential.
What's included in the shower facility?
Our shower facility is clean, private, and well-stocked with fresh towels and basic toiletries. You're welcome to use it before or after your treatment.
Are you really open 24 hours?
Yes! We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week. However, we recommend booking in advance for late-night or early-morning slots to ensure therapist availability.
Can I choose which services to include in my package?
Yes! Both packages include service choices. The Basic includes 1 service, Premium includes 2. You can choose from our full service menu — body waxing, massage types, and more.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash (PKR), Easypaisa, JazzCash, and bank transfer. Please confirm at the time of booking. Payment is made at the spa before the session begins.
